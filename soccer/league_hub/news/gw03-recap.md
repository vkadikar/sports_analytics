## The Results

**FC Purulona 121.75 – 46.75 Smip Estonian**  
Sweet Mother of Jesus, call the Old Bill because there’s been an absolute murder on the pitch. Smip Estonian rolled into the weekend second in the table thinking they were the bollocks after dropping 142 points last week, only to get their trousers pulled down in front of everyone. Kai Havertz (21.5) and Gianluigi Donnarumma (17.25) led a complete riot for Purulona, while Smip’s lot fell apart like a cheap motor—Jordan Pickford finished on -2.0 and half their outfield lads couldn't crack two points. Seventy-five point pasting. Utterly humiliating.

**Wallalujah 114.25 – 77.0 Estimated Profit**  
Wallalujah keep marching on at the summit, and they didn’t even need their bench to stick the boot in. Tyrick Mitchell went absolutely berserk from left-back with 25.25 points, backed up by Marcus Tavernier (21.5) and Zion Suzuki (18.25) between the sticks. Estimated Profit had Erling Haaland doing Erling Haaland things with 19.0 points, but when you're starting Emiliano Buendia (1.0) and Maxence Lacroix (2.5) while leaving 32 points on your pine, you deserve to get battered.

**sduvuuru 108.25 – 47.0 Traderjoe18**  
Talk about getting off the mark in style. sduvuuru finally remembered how to play fantasy football, driven by masterclasses from Alex Scott (21.5) and Enzo Le Fée (15.5) in the middle of the park. Traderjoe18 was a total shambles from back to front—Alisson Becker pulled off 18.5 points in goal, but Adrien Truffert (-0.75) scored in reverse, Eberechi Eze didn't even put his boots on, and they limped to a pathetic 47.0.

**youngmoon 105.0 – 84.5 seanbridges7**  
The clash of the unbeatens turned into a proper heavyweight dust-up, but youngmoon had way too much artillery in the end. Martin Odegaard (18.5), Luke Shaw (14.75), and Pedro Porro (14.5) piled on the agony, easily overcoming Igor Jesus’s pathetic cameo (-0.5). Seanbridges7 fought like dogs behind Alexander Isak (23.5) and Harvey Barnes (19.0), but getting doughnuts from Riccardo Calafiori (0.0) killed their unbeaten run stone dead.

**MOO FC 92.5 – 70.0 Birds & Bees FC**  
MOO FC bounced back nicely, mostly thanks to Cody Gakpo running riot for 24.0 points and Antoine Semenyo chipping in a tasty 15.0. Birds & Bees FC looked like they’d had ten pints before kickoff; Djordje Petrovic dropped a stinker in goal (-0.75), Bradley Barcola barely moved for his 3.0, and Eddie Nketiah’s 12.5 wasn't nearly enough to keep them buzzing.

**FC VAR 83.5 – 54.25 WayneRooney10**  
FC VAR maintained their 100% record despite trying their absolute best to throw it away by leaving a small army of points on the bench. Christos Tzolis (16.0), Vitaly Janelt (15.0), and James McAtee (15.0) did just enough to see off a dreadful WayneRooney10 side that started two blokes who went negative—Patrick Dorgu (-1.25) and Mateus Fernandes (-2.0)—plus a ghost in Nico O'Reilly (0.0). Bryan Mbeumo (17.0) must be wondering what sin he committed to play with those clowns.

**Arnie-senal 70.0 – 61.25 Toadenham Frogspur**  
An absolute eyesore of a match that felt like watching paint dry in a gale. Arnie-senal scraped their first win of the season thanks to Jan-Paul van Hecke (12.5) and Nordi Mukiele (11.25), completely surviving Mikel Merino coming on for 13 minutes just to grab a yellow and post -2.0. Toadenham had Marc Guehi (15.0) and Morgan Rogers (12.5), but Levi Colwill didn't play a single second and Adam Wharton produced a measly 1.5. Proper garbage, but Arnie won't care.
